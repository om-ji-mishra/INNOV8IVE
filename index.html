The 6-Stage Learning Pathway

A comprehensive pipeline bridging foundational mechanics with embedded systems, ROS 2 robotics, and autonomous systems.

STAGE 01 Ages 9–11

Explorer

Demystify hardware through tangible play, sensory discovery, and simple algorithmic cause-and-effect.

  • DC Circuits & LED Logic
  • Block-based flow control
  • Obstacle Avoidance Buggy
STAGE 02 Ages 11–14

Builder

Bridge software logic with discrete circuit hardware, transitioning from block code to structured syntax.

  • Arduino & C++ Syntax
  • Transistors & Regulators
  • 4-DOF Robotic Arm
STAGE 03 Ages 14–16

Maker

Master custom printed circuit boards, parametric 3D fabrication, and real-time sensor fusion.

  • Fusion 360 & DFM Slicing
  • KiCad 2-Layer PCB Layout
  • Inverted Pendulum Self-Balancer
STAGE 04 Ages 16–18

Engineer

Engineer robust embedded architectures, deterministic communication protocols, and industrial mechanics.

  • STM32 ARM & FreeRTOS
  • CAN Bus & Industrial SPI
  • Custom Flight Controller Board
STAGE 05 Ages 18–21

Technologist

Implement distributed autonomous systems using ROS 2, LiDAR SLAM, and edge neural processing.

  • ROS 2 Humble/Iron Nodes & Nav2
  • 2D/3D LiDAR SLAM & MoveIt 2
  • Autonomous AMR & Jetson Orin
STAGE 06 Ages 21–24

Innovator

Transform bleeding-edge robotics research into venture-backed deep-tech hardware products and patentable IP.

  • Quadruped Dynamic Locomotion RL
  • CE/FCC Compliance & DFM
  • Commercial Deep-Tech Products
